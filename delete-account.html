Delete Your LifeCapsule Account

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by following these steps:

Option 1 — In-app: Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all stories immediately.
Option 2 — By email: Send a deletion request to momerrafiq01@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and your registered email address. We will process it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile information
  • All stories and photos you have uploaded
  • Your connections and activity history

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
