Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Welcome to LifeCapsule. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Email address and password when you register.
  • User content: Life stories, memories, photos, and audio recordings you choose to create and store in the app.
  • Device permissions: We request access to your microphone (for audio recordings) and photo library (for uploading images) only when you use those features.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the LifeCapsule service.
  • To store and display your memories and life stories.
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To improve the app based on usage patterns.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud infrastructure provider. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law or to operate the service (e.g., cloud storage providers).

5. Microphone and Photo Access

LifeCapsule requests access to your microphone and photo library solely to allow you to create audio recordings and upload images for your personal memories. We do not access these features without your explicit action.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

LifeCapsule is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
